About

Parent led. For our kids.

Senn Parents is the shared front door for every parent and community group at Nicholas Senn High School. Each group runs itself, sets its own agenda, and elects its own leaders. This site is how families find them, hear from them, and show up.

You do not need to be a member to attend a meeting, ask a question, or bring an idea.
